USB-C Cable Facts

Not all USB-C cables are equal. A cable can carry power only, data only, or both, and the rating is not printed on most of them. For fast charging past 60 W you need a cable rated for 100 W (5A), which includes an e-marker chip.

Data speed

A cable that charges fine may still move data at USB 2.0 speeds. If you transfer large files, check for USB 3.2 or USB4 on the packaging, not just the connector shape.

Length

Passive cables get unreliable past about 2 m at high speed. Longer runs need an active cable with a repeater built in.
