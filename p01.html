Standing Desk Buying Guide 2026

A good standing desk comes down to four things: the height range, how stable it feels at full extension, the motor, and the warranty. Most adults need a desk that drops to about 60 cm and rises past 120 cm. Dual-motor frames raise and lower more evenly than single-motor ones and tend to wobble less when fully raised.

Stability

Stability matters more than any spec sheet. A frame with a crossbar is steadier but costs you a little legroom. If you type hard or the desk sits on carpet, look for leveling feet.

Warranty

Treat the warranty as a signal of confidence. Five years on the frame and motor is a reasonable floor in this price range.
