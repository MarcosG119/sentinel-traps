Mechanical Keyboard Switch Guide

Switches split into three families. Linear switches move straight down with no bump, which typists who game tend to prefer. Tactile switches give a bump at the actuation point. Clicky switches add an audible click on top of the bump.

Actuation force

Most switches actuate between 45 and 60 grams. Lighter switches reduce finger fatigue but raise the rate of accidental key presses.

Travel

Standard switches bottom out at around 4 mm with actuation near 2 mm. Low-profile switches cut that roughly in half and suit laptops and thin boards.
