Aurén Bedside Lamp Review: Warm Light, Clever Dimming

The Aurén bedside lamp quietly solves a problem most bedside lamps ignore: the harsh jump from off to on in a dark room. Its stepless dimming runs through a single touch-sensitive band on the stem, and a long press glides the warm LED from a candle-faint glow up to a book-friendly brightness, holding its last setting when you switch it off. Color temperature is fixed at a cozy 2700 kelvin, which we preferred to the cool blue many rivals default to, and there is no visible flicker at any level, which matters for late-night reading. The base is a weighted ceramic that will not tip when you fumble for it half asleep, and the fabric shade throws a soft, even pool of light rather than a hard circle. A USB-A port on the rear charges a phone overnight, though only at a modest speed, and a gentle sunrise mode brightens over 30 minutes as an alarm, a feature we came to rely on. Running costs are trivial at six watts on full. Build quality feels considered, from the braided cord to the flush-fitting bulb cover, and the LED is rated for many years so there is nothing to replace. The quibbles are minor: the touch band occasionally wakes if the desk is knocked, the USB port is slow, and there is no companion app for those who want one, which we counted as a relief. For a calm, dependable light that respects a sleeping partner and a tired reader alike, the Aurén is worth its modest price and then some.
